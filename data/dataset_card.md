## ⚠️ What Is Synthetic — Stated Plainly

The following components are **synthetic** (not derived from real patient observations):

1. **EHR records** — Generated by Synthea (or our Python fallback), calibrated toward Indian T2D demographics but NOT Indian patient data.
2. **EHR ↔ wearable pairing** — Each synthetic EHR is matched to a real CGMacros participant by nearest-neighbour on diabetes status, age band, BMI band, and HbA1c band. The pairing is *statistical*, not clinical.
3. **Indian meal substitutions** — ~30% of CGMacros meals are replaced with Indian meals matched by carbohydrate load. The glucose response is from the *original* CGMacros participant eating a *different* meal, so the food→glucose mapping is assumed, not measured.
4. **Genetic risk score** — Simulated from a Beta(2,3) distribution; no real genotyping.

The model is a **proof of concept** for the fusion architecture and system design, **not** evidence of clinical performance on Indian patients.

## Data Sources

### CGMacros (PhysioNet)
- **URL:** https://www.physionet.org/content/cgmacros/
- **Paper:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12462512
- **Licence:** Listed as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 in the paper. **Verify at PhysioNet** — the licence file appeared empty when checked during preparation. The NonCommercial clause may affect prize eligibility.
- **Contents:**
  - Continuous glucose monitor (CGM) readings at ~5-min intervals
  - Fitbit intraday heart rate
  - Fitbit activity/step counts
  - Fitbit sleep logs
  - Self-reported meal logs with macronutrients (calories, carbs, protein, fat)
  - Baseline labs (HbA1c, fasting glucose, lipids)
  - Demographics (age, sex, BMI, diabetes status)
- **Population:** 45 free-living adults in the US (15 healthy, 16 prediabetes, 14 T2D), ~10 days each.
- **Limitations:** Small, US-only, no Indian foods, self-reported meals.

### Synthea (Apache 2.0)
- **URL:** https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0 — no restrictions.
- **Usage:** Generate synthetic Type 2 Diabetes EHR records.
- **India calibration:** Age, BMI, HbA1c, and lipid distributions shifted to match Indian T2D prevalence data (WHO, ICMR INDIAB). This shifts output distributions but does not make the records clinically Indian.

### Indian Food Database (Curated)
- **Location:** `data/indian_food_db.csv`
- **Size:** ~200 common Indian food items
- **Columns:** food_id, food_name, category, portion_unit, portion_grams, calories, carbs, protein, fat, fibre, glycemic_index, glycemic_load, is_vegetarian, preparation_modifier
- **Source:** Values assembled from published Indian nutrition composition data. Licence of source tables (IFCT/INDB) not yet independently verified — noted as a risk.
- **Limitation:** Small (200 items) vs. commercial databases (37,000+ items). Our value is transparency and auditability, not coverage.

## Pairing Method

| Step | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Algorithm** | Weighted Euclidean nearest-neighbour |
| **Match features** | Diabetes status (weight=10), HbA1c (2), BMI (1.5), Age (1) |
| **Normalisation** | Z-score across combined populations |
| **Reuse policy** | Same CGMacros participant may match multiple EHR patients (N_ehr > N_cgmacros) |
| **Audit log** | `data/pairing_log.csv` — every match with distance and band assignments |
| **Seed** | 42 (configurable) |

---

## Augmentation Method

- **Fraction:** 30% of meals replaced (configurable)
- **Matching:** Indian meals selected by carbohydrate proximity (±40% tolerance) to original meal, stratified by meal type
- **Scaling:** If no close match, portions are scaled proportionally to hit the target carb load
- **Labelling:** Every augmented row has `is_augmented=True`, `augmentation_source="indian_meal_substitution"`, and the original macros preserved in `original_carbs_g` / `original_calories_kcal`
- **Distribution report:** `data/processed/augmentation_distribution.csv`

## Feature Windows

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| History length | 4 hours (48 steps at 5-min) |
| Prediction horizon | 2 hours (24 steps) |
| Stride | 15 minutes (configurable) |
| Min glucose coverage | 75% of history must be non-NaN |
| Targets | Glucose at 30, 60, 120 min; spike >180 mg/dL; hypo <70 mg/dL |

### Feature groups per window:
- **Glucose (11):** mean, std, min, max, range, last, RoC-5, RoC-15, CV, trend, NaN fraction
- **Heart rate (5):** mean, std, min, max, resting proxy (10th percentile)
- **Activity (4):** total steps, mean per interval, intensity proxy, active fraction
- **Sleep (4):** last duration, hours ago, efficiency, slept-last-night flag
- **Meal (6):** recent GL, recent carbs, recent calories, time since meal, cumulative carbs 4h, meal count 4h
- **EHR static (16):** age, sex, BMI, HbA1c, fasting glucose, lipids (4), genetic risk, diabetes duration, comorbidities (5)

## Reproducibility

The entire dataset can be regenerated from scratch:

```bash
python scripts/build_dataset.py --seed 42
```

All random operations use the specified seed. The demo mode (when CGMacros is not yet downloaded) generates a self-contained synthetic dataset for development.

---

## Known Limitations

1. **Only 45 real participants** — metrics must be reported with bootstrap confidence intervals
2. **US cohort only** — no real Indian CGM data
3. **Self-reported meals** — food logging accuracy varies
4. **Pairing is statistical** — a synthetic EHR assigned to a real participant does not mean those lab values belong to that person
5. **Indian meal glucose response is assumed** — the glucose trace is from the original meal, not the substituted Indian meal
6. **Food DB is small** — 200 items vs. thousands in commercial products
